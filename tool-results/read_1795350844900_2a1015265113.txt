    10→## 1. Executive summary
    11→
    12→The Octavox plug-in's pitch engine is a module named `dtonicii9` whose DSP core is the class
    13→`modpp::modules::dtonicii_t::dsp_t`, embedded at `module+0x430` and reachable through the vtable
    14→`modpp::modules::dtonicii_t::dsp_t::vftable`. The engine is a **multi-voice, variable-rate delay-line
    15→pitch shifter driven by an on-board periodicity tracker** that emits *discrete interval records*
    16→rather than a continuous pitch.
    17→
    18→The chain, in one sentence per stage:
    19→
    20→1. **Input framing** — 4 samples per internal block are pushed into a power-of-two history ring whose
    21→   index increases with age; the newest sample is at the current write index and reading
    22→   `ring[(write + d) & mask]` returns the sample `d` samples ago. (**OBS**)
    23→2. **Periodicity analysis** — a single tracker instance decimates the input to ≈12 kHz with a
    24→   symmetric low-pass FIR, applies an adaptive gate and an AGC, and runs a 10-state machine that
    25→   performs 48-tap lag-difference correlation over 243 candidate lags, refines the peak
    26→   parabolically, and emits 4-float records `{period, i5, i4, correction}` into a 4096-float ring.
    27→   (**OBS**)
    28→3. **Interval selection** — each voice independently reads the record ring (biased by a per-voice
    29→   read offset) and turns the record into a *transposition interval* (`i4`), a *rate factor*
    30→   (`i5`/record slot), and a *period correction* (`correction`) that also rescales the period by a
    31→   power of two. (**OBS**)
    32→4. **Voice generation** — per voice: a linear gain ramp windows and delays the input into a
    33→   per-voice work ring; a 6-point, phase-quantised interpolator reads that ring at a
    34→   continuously advancing rate; the read offset follows a saw-tooth whose re-arm jump is masked by a
    35→   two-tap crossfade. A geometric glide with separate up/down rates smooths interval changes.
    36→   (**OBS** structure; **DER** interpretation)
    37→5. **Control graph** — everything that is not audio is a lock-free, dirty-bit "pull" graph of nodes
    38→   (`rdm slew`, `c_multiply`, `c_adder8`, `scal table`, `moddelay`) that publish into named engine
    39→   ports (`shared_t` / `sharedvector_t` / `sharedarray_t`), plus a per-voice Chamberlin state-variable
    40→   filter that generates modulation signals. (**OBS**)
    41→6. **Output** — voice outputs go to per-voice output ports; the summation / wet-dry / stereo stage
    42→   was not reached in this round. (**UNK**)

    46→## 2. Reference pseudo-implementation (reconstructed behaviour)
    47→
    48→```
    49→engine.prepare(block):                        # per 4-sample sub-block
    50→    push_4(block) into IN_RING(age-indexed, mask = 2^k - 1)
    51→    tracker.run(block[1], block[3])           # 2 of every 4 samples
    52→
    53→    for v in 0..nvoices-1:
    54→        g[v] = clamp(target_gain[v] - g[v], -1, 0)
    55→        for k in 0..3:
    56→            WORK[v][(W[v]-k) & wmask] = -(g[v] * IN_RING[(D[v]+k') & mask])
    57→        W[v] -= 4
    58→
    59→        x   = ctrl_in[v] * page[v][10]
    60→        rate= quartic(x) * rate_slot[v] * 64            # samples / sample
    61→        pg  = pageA if selector[v] != 1 else pageB
    62→
    63→        for k in 0..3:                                   # one output sample each
    64→            pos[v] -= rate
    65→            if pos[v] < 0: pos[v] = prev
    66→            tapA = interp6(WORK[v], (int)pos[v])
    67→            tapB = interp6(WORK[v], (int)pos[v] - D[v])
    68→            out[v] = tapA + (tapA - tapB) * xf_prev[v]
    69→        xf[v] = max(xf[v] - corr[v], -1)
    70→
    71→        if (int)xf[v] == -1:
    72→            idx    = (read_index[v] + tracker_write) & 0xFFF
    73→            period = clamp(REC[idx] * 2^-11, 48, 480)
    74→            i4     = int(REC[(idx+1) & 0xFFF])           # 0 if pitch tracking off
    75→            rate_slot[v] = page[v][0] * REC[(idx+2) & 0xFFF]
    76→            col    = clamp((period-48)/16, 0, 31)
    77→            c      = page[v][col+12]
    78→            corr[v]= c * 2^-23
    79→            period = 2^(c & 0xF) * period + 0.5
    80→            if (delay-commit condition):
    81→                int_delay[v] = (int)(pos[v] - int_delay[v]); xf[v] = 0
    82→
    83→        page[v][1] = page[v][i4+2]                       # target ratio (interval table)
    84→        page[v][0] = glide(page[v][0], page[v][1], up=engine[0x44e0], down=engine[0x44f8])
    85→```
    86→
    87→`interp6` = 6 taps from a 256-row × 6-float table, selected by
    88→`((int)(pos * 2048) & 0x7F8) >> 3`.

   108→## 4. Structural summary of the recovered design
   109→
   110→* **One tracker, many voices.** A single analysis instance (`engine+0x4518`) feeds up to 8 voices
   111→  through a shared 4096-float record ring; per-voice divergence comes from a per-voice read offset
   112→  (port 24) and a per-voice page (ports 21/22). (**OBS**)
   113→* **Interval, not frequency.** The tracker emits `{period, i5, i4, correction}`; `i4` indexes a
   114→  per-voice interval table. This is the structural reason the plug-in is "diatonic"/"harmonising"
   115→  rather than a plain harmoniser. (**OBS/DER**)
   116→* **Table-driven everywhere.** Phase interpolation uses a 256×6 table; the period→correction map is a
   117→  32-entry column of the per-voice page; the scale gate is an `int[12]`. Nothing on the audio path
   118→  was found to compute a trigonometric function at run time. (**OBS**)
   119→* **No lock on the control path.** All control sharing is flag-bit based (`LOCK OR` to publish,
   120→  `LOCK CMPXCHG` to clear). (**OBS**)
   121→* **Two parallel worker trees.** The whole worker tree exists twice; the purpose is unresolved.
   122→  (**OBS/UNK**) The module graph, however, contains exactly **one** graph per IO profile, so the two
   123→  trees are not the channel branches (D1 §6.0, D7 Q-1).
   124→* **The engine is one node in a 395-module patch.** The complete graph is recovered verbatim
   125→  (D1 §6): input gain → mono fold → anti-alias IIR → soft clip → `dtonicii9` (8 voices) → per-voice
   126→  HP/EQ/de-clicking-delay/feedback → 8-into-stereo mixer with level and pan → global feedback with
   127→  DC blocker and 20 ms crossfade → wet/dry (`mmix`) → output gain → hard bypass selector, plus eight
   128→  random-modulation chains and the metering/LED taps. (**OBS**)

   265→### 4g. Round-8 verification pass (2026-10-21) — rate-map constants, host-latency path
   266→
   267→* **Rate map: constants and precision fixed.** The five polynomial coefficients are **immediates in
   268→  the engine constructor** (`0x1800E1F82–0x1800E1FB4`, `C7 86 <disp32> <imm32>`), not `.rdata`
   269→  loads: `0x3E800000` 0.25, `0x3EB12E86` 0.346058071, `0x3E79C4BB` 0.24391453, `0x3DF569C6`
   270→  0.119830653, `0x3D0ED15E` 0.0348676369 (sum **0.994670891**). `P` is evaluated in double from the float32
   271→  coefficients widened with `CVTPS2PD` (`0F 5A`) and stays double through both multiplier steps (`f` is
   272→  the `MULSD` product at `0x1800E236D`; the constant is the double `64.0` at `0x180412490`); there is
   273→  no float32 rounding of `P` itself. (D6 §10.5, R-36)
   274→* **Unity is a locus, not a pair.** `step = 1` ⇔ `P(f)·R = 1/64` ⇒ `R = 1/(64·P(f))`; `P(0)=0.25`,
   275→  `P(1)=0.994670891`, `P(f)=1` at `f=1.003988571`, so `R ≤ 0.0625` for every admissible `f`. If the
   276→  binding is `R = ratio/64` the map is −9.25 cents at `f=1` and exactly unity at `f=1.003989`. The
   277→  two slots are per-voice field offsets installed at construction (`0x1800E7EF6`/`0x1800E7F0E`), so
   278→  their values are graph-dependent; Q-7 stays **PARTIAL**. (D6 §10.5, R-37)

   298→### 4h. Round-8b: gate units closed in the audio path (Q-15/C5)
   299→
   300→* **The gate routine is one chunked routine; the gate is in case 6.** `functions.csv` splits it at
   301→  `0x18013BCDA` and `0x18013C85E` although control flows linearly through both boundaries. The switch
   302→  `movsxd rax,[r15+0x5FF8]` (0..9) dispatches through the table at `0x18013D22C`; case 6 =
   303→  `0x18013CF3C`, case 7 = `0x18013D127`, so the block `0x18013D0C0-0x18013D0F2` lies in case 6, and a
   304→  whole-`.text` branch scan finds only three (local) entries into it. The round-6 "state 6"
   305→  attribution is **verified**, not assumed. (R-39)
   306→* **The compared quantity is a power envelope, so the domain is consistent.** The shared prologue
   307→  computes `S[+4] = (1/128)·x² + (127/128)·S[+4]` with constructor immediates (`0x3C000000`,
   308→  `0x3F7E0000` at `0x1800E0E1E`/`0x1800E0E14`); `c/(1-d) = 1`, so it is a normalised one-pole
   309→  **power** envelope. The gate then executes
   310→  `if ([T+0x5FD0] - S[+4] < FLT_EPSILON) S[0] = new`: **power vs power**, and the semantic is
   311→  *update above the threshold / hold below* (freeze-on-silence), not a mute. At the shipped `-55`
   312→  the threshold is `3.16e-6`, so the branch fires for any level above it - the round-7 statement
   313→  "the branch would never fire" is **retracted**, as is the "dB-domain" label. (R-40)
   314→* **The applier is byte-exact.** `min(1, exp(clamp(g, [+0x1F0], [+0x1F4]) · 0.23025850929))` with
   315→  `0x180412250` = `ln 10/10` (11 digits), `exp` at `0x180351F50`, cap `1.0` at `0x18041213C`, sent as
   316→  parameter 2. Sentinels: `-99` -> parameter 4 = `2/3`, flag 0; `-98` -> `0`, flag 1; `-97` -> `2/3`,
   317→  flag 1. (R-40)
   318→* **The residual unit error is localised to `m02` params 12/13.** `FUN_1800E1520` dispatches on
   319→  `edx-7` (table `0x1800E1890`); parameter 12 = `0x1800E1751`, parameter 13 = `0x1800E17E1`. Both
   320→  apply `10^(x/20)` (constant `0.11513` at `0x180412200`) to the **already-linear power**, compare
   321→  the result with `sqrt(S[+4]·0.99999988)`, and emit `-1731.234·ln(corr)`, i.e. `1200·log2(1/corr)`
   322→  **cents**. With a normalised envelope (`sqrt(S[+4]) ≤ 1`) and a threshold `≥ 1.0`, those outputs
   323→  are **0 for every normalised signal** - a DER prediction, conditional on normalisation and not
   324→  runtime-verified. This is also the correction of the earlier "dB-like" unit label for `m02`
   325→  0xc/0xd. (R-41)
   326→* **The parameter's module entry point is now RTTI-confirmed.** `FUN_1800E10B0` is slot 1 and
   327→  `FUN_1800E1520` slot 2 of the `.?AUdsp_t@dtonicii_t@modules@modpp@@` vtable `0x180409F60`
   328→  (COL `0x180435D08`, installed at `0x1800E03BA`), and the 18-entry table at `0x1800E14D0` maps
   329→  **index 2 -> `0x1800E110F`** -> `mov [rbx+0xA4E8],eax`. The default path's `[vptr+0x18]` is slot 3
   330→  (`0x1800C5750` -> `modules[edx]->slot1(id,value*)`), not a self call. What stays **UNK** is the
   331→  applier's module index `[applier+8]`, a runtime value. (R-42)

   335→  unproven (applier on the parameter path, voice routine on the audio path). (D6 §10.8)

   386→## 6. Validation plan, and separation of evidence from our own implementation
   387→
   388→* **Static evidence** (this document set, `Evdence\*.md`, `OX_RESEARCH\analysis\*`): all statements
   389→  are code/byte observations of the target and are traceable to a function address and, where
   390→  relevant, to a line of an extracted pseudocode file.
   391→* **Our own implementation** (`Evdence\poc\`): our independent DSP and its measurement harness.
   392→  Its numbers describe **our** implementation only and are never presented as target measurements.
   393→  It contains `octavox_poc.py` (delay-line DSP + synthetic harness, `poc_measurements.json` /
   394→  `POC_RESULTS.md`) and `octavox_offline.py` (real WAV→WAV CLI, two further pitch-shift designs and
   395→  the shared comparison runner, `offline_measurements.json` / `OFFLINE_COMPARISON.md`).
   396→* **Measurements produced by the harness** (each repeatable, fixed seeds, fixed sample rate):
   397→  1. pitch accuracy per voice — expected vs measured fundamental in cents, reported **three** ways:
   398→     the YIN (cumulative-mean-normalised difference) grain-average period, the parabolic-interpolated
   399→     spectral peak (instantaneous carrier), and a closed-form model of the crossfade artefact;
   400→  2. latency (impulse onset, samples and ms);
   401→  3. transient behaviour (attack to −6 dB both absolutely and relative to the delayed burst's
   402→     appearance, pre-ring, residual 20 ms after the *slow* tap has drained);
   403→  4. discontinuities / spectral cleanliness (`max |Δy|`, `max |Δ²y|` against the smooth-sine baseline,
   404→     out-of-harmonic energy fraction) for three interpolators: 6-point Lagrange, 256-phase quantised
   405→     Lagrange, linear;
   406→  5. cost (realtime factor and ms CPU per second of audio, 1 and 8 voices);
   407→  6. stability (boundedness, non-finite count, 4 s run, 0.35 feedback, 8 voices);
   408→  7. a grain-length sweep (G = 512…16384) exposing the accuracy-versus-latency trade-off;
   409→  8. unity-ratio behaviour (the two-tap comb at `r = 1`) against its closed form.
   410→
   411→### 6b. Headline results of our own implementation (not target measurements)
   412→
   413→| quantity | value (G = 4096, 8 voices, 48 kHz, pure Python) |
   414→|---|---|
   415→| pitch error, all 8 voices | ≤ **6.6 cents** (YIN); ≤ 8.9 cents on the instantaneous carrier |
   416→| ≥ G = 2048, out-of-harmonic energy | **0.0000** (13.2 % at G = 512 — the phase-modulation artefact) |
   417→| latency | **2303 samples = 48.0 ms** (= `base_delay + G/2`) |
   418→| transient attack after the delayed burst appears | **63 samples ≈ 1.3 ms**; pre-ring and post-drain residual **−228 dB** (numerically silent) |
   419→| smoothness | `max |Δ²y|` **equals** the smooth-sine baseline exactly (−61.6 dB re peak) → no crossfade clicks |
   420→| unity ratio | **−2.42 dB** measured vs −2.42 dB analytic `|cos(π f G / sr)|` (two-tap comb) |
   421→| stability, 8 voices, feedback 0.35, 4 s | bounded, 0 non-finite blocks, `max |y| = 0.25` |
   422→| cost | 0.31× realtime (1 voice), 0.17× (8 voices) — CPython, no optimisation attempted |
   423→
   424→The grain sweep shows measured and modelled error agreeing in sign and magnitude in every regime
   425→(−41.89 vs −41.18 cents at G = 512/+3 st; +65.61 vs +65.23 at G = 512/−4 st; +4.09 vs +3.32 at
   426→G = 4096; +0.74 vs −0.34 at G = 16384) and both approaching zero as `1/G` while latency grows
   427→linearly. This is the intended, honest cross-check: the model is not fitted to the measurement, the
   428→same closed form was derived independently and reproduces it.
   429→* **A measured property of the reconstructed architecture.** The two-tap complementary crossfade is
   430→  amplitude-exact but not phase-exact: over one grain the mix coefficient rotates the carrier phase by
   431→  `ΔΦ = Φ(1)−Φ(0)`, so the output fundamental moves by `ΔΦ·(1−ratio)/G·sr/(2π)`. The harness measures
   432→  this *and* predicts it from the closed form; measured and modelled agree in sign and magnitude in
   433→  every tested regime (−41.89 vs −41.18 cents at G=512/+3; +65.61 vs +65.23 at G=512/−4; +4.09 vs
   434→  +3.32 at G=4096/+3; +0.74 vs −0.34 at G=16384/+3). The artefact falls as ≈1/G while latency rises
   435→  linearly — a genuine design trade-off, not an implementation defect. See `poc/README.md`.
   436→* **A second measured structural consequence (ours).** At `ratio = 1` the grain phase stops
   437→  advancing, the crossfade freezes at `w = 0.5`, and the two taps (G apart) are both read: the unison
   438→  voice is a **comb**, not a copy. Measured −2.42 dB at 220 Hz with G = 4096, exactly the analytic
   439→  `|cos(ω₀G/2)|`; notch spacing `sr/G`. This yields a falsifiable prediction about the target (a clean
   440→  unison would require the tap distance to vary at `r = 1`), recorded as Q-14 — it is *not* a claim
   441→  about the target, which was never observed running.
   442→* **Runtime behaviour of the target** remains unmeasured. After round 6 the only item that still
   443→  needs a runtime trace on the target itself is Q-14 (the audible two-tap comb at unity); the
   444→  read-rate sign (Q-7) is proven statically, and the host latency (Q-8) is an unresolved negative
   445→  rather than a runtime question.

   447→### 6c. Round-6 comparison of three independent designs (ours, identical inputs)
   448→
   449→Measured by `poc/octavox_offline.py --compare` at 48 kHz, ratio 1.25 and 0.80, on deterministic
   450→signals (`OCTAVOX_ROUND6_MEASUREMENTS.md` has the full tables and metric definitions):
   451→
   452→| ratio | design | pitch err (cents) | latency (ms) | onset delay (ms) | attack (ms) | envelope modulation (dB) | cost, 8 voices (ms/s) |
   453→|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
   454→| 1.25 | delay line | +3.58 | 102.5 | 56.2 | 130.4 | 15.7 | 6504 |
   455→| 1.25 | phase vocoder | +0.03 | 34.2 | 14.4 | 8.2 | 1.1 | 181 |
   456→| 1.25 | transient-aware PV | +0.02 | 36.4 | 20.4 | 5.5 | 0.8 | 191 |
   457→| 0.80 | delay line | −4.59 | 65.2 | 15.6 | 98.3 | 16.8 | 6566 |
   458→| 0.80 | phase vocoder | +0.02 | 53.2 | 27.0 | 26.0 | 3.3 | 174 |
   459→| 0.80 | transient-aware PV | +0.01 | 50.6 | 28.9 | 8.5 | 3.3 | 189 |
   460→
   461→**Decision from the measurements:** the next direction is the **transient-aware phase vocoder**
   462→(short analysis window + phase re-lock at detected onsets). It wins every measured axis — pitch
   463→accuracy, latency, attack, amplitude modulation and out-of-harmonic energy — while costing ~30× less
   464→CPU than the Python delay line. The delay line remains the faithful reconstruction of the target
   465→*architecture* and is retained as the reference design and as the regression baseline for the
   466→synthetic harness; its measured latency is not constant (48 ms at unison, 65 ms at 0.80, 102 ms at
   467→1.25), which is itself a property of the recovered design. No claim is made that the target sounds
   468→like either of the new designs.
